# Expenses
## Receipts
A receipt is required for an expense of $25 or more. Expenses below $25 do not require a receipt.
## Submission
Submit expense reimbursement requests within 30 days of the expense date.
## Eligibility
Approved business meals and travel expenses are eligible. Personal entertainment is ineligible.

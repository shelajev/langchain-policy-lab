# Travel
## Meal allowance
Meals are reimbursed up to the daily meal allowance, not a separate dinner limit.
Tier 1: $90 per day. Tier 2: $70 per day. Tier 3: $50 per day.
Employees must use the approved city tier list; this demo does not supply real cities.
## Booking
Book airfare at least 14 days before departure. Economy airfare is required.
## Lodging
The lodging limit is $180 per night, excluding approved taxes.

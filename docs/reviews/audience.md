# Reader brief

Reader: a Python developer or AI platform engineer evaluating an internal
support-assistant learning lab. Assume familiarity with Python, a terminal,
Git, containers and JSON, but no prior knowledge of this project, Kits v3,
uv conventions, sbx environment files or the distinction between fake-model
and live-provider validation. The reader needs to follow the complete guide
from a fresh checkout, understand limits and judge the factual marketing
claims. The outline is a proposal with author gates pending.
